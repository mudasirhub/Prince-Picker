Prince Picker Feature Catalog

Every capability in Prince Picker is built for high-speed counter billing, multi-location stock tracking, vehicle compatibility mapping, and offline stability.

1. Product & SKU Catalog

Comprehensive part cataloging designed for two-wheeler and automotive retailers:

  • Unique Barcode / SKU mapping per item.
  • MRP and Selling Price (SP) tracking with discount calculations.
  • Up to 4 optimized product image slots per part.
  • Categorization by Brand, Category, and Part Type.

2. Vehicle Model Compatibility & Fitments

Eliminate part mismatch by mapping items directly to vehicle models:

  • Tag parts with compatible models (Honda, Hero, Bajaj, TVS, Yamaha, Royal Enfield).
  • Group interchangeable parts into fitment groups.
  • Instant compatibility lookup at counter sales.

3. Camera Barcode Scanning

Hardware-free camera scanning for fast operations:

  • Native camera integration on mobile phones, tablets, and webcams.
  • Instant product scan to cart at checkout.
  • Rapid inventory counts and barcode verification.

4. Shop & Warehouse Location Chips

Structured Rack/Level/Bin location tracking:

  • `SHOP` locations formatted as Rack/Level/Bin (`R1L1B1`).
  • `WAREHOUSE` locations formatted with `WR` prefix (`WR1L1B1`).
  • Location chips with distinct quantities (`[R1L1B1 · 10 pcs · Primary]`).
  • Per-location stock alert thresholds.

5. Sales Checkout & Credit Ledger

Point of Sale billing and customer credit management:

  • Fast checkout with receipt printing capability.
  • Customer credit ledger tracking for mechanics and workshops.
  • Partial payment logging and balance reminders.

6. Stock Transfers & Pick/Drop Workflows

Seamless stock movement between locations:

  • Transfer items between shop floor and warehouse storage.
  • Warehouse pick workflow routing for order fulfillment.
  • Drop workflow for inbound part restocking.

7. Offline-First PWA Architecture

Full operational resilience during network failure:

  • Local IndexedDB & Cache storage guarantees zero latency.
  • Operate checkout and inventory lookup 100% offline.
  • Automatic background synchronization when network reconnects.
