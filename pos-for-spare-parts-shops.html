POS Software for Two-Wheeler Spare-Parts Shops

Managing a two-wheeler spare-parts business requires handling thousands of part numbers, vehicle compatibility mappings, fast counter checkout, customer credit ledgers, and multi-location stock controls.

Why Generic POS Systems Struggle with Spare-Parts Inventory

Generic retail Point of Sale systems are built for simple retail items with single barcodes and fixed prices. Automotive spare parts shops face distinct challenges:

  • Enormous SKU Inventories: A single shop often stocks over 5,000 unique SKUs across cables, brake pads, clutches, filters, gaskets, and engine components.
  • Vehicle Model Compatibility: Customers ask for parts based on vehicle model (e.g. "Hero Splendor BS6 brake cable" or "Honda Activa 5G air filter"). Generic POS systems do not map parts to vehicle models.
  • Multiple Storage Locations: Fast-moving items sit on counter shelves, while bulk inventory resides in secondary warehouse racks. Generic POS systems track total quantity but lose rack-level location visibility.

How Prince Picker Solves Spare-Parts Counter & Inventory Workflows

Prince Picker is designed around the actual daily operational flow of automotive spare parts counters:

  • Vehicle Model Search & Fitment Groups: Search items by two-wheeler model (Honda Activa, Hero Splendor, Bajaj Pulsar, TVS Jupiter, Royal Enfield Classic) or fitment groups.
  • Camera-Based Barcode Scanning: Scan part barcodes instantly using smartphone or tablet cameras without buying specialized scanner hardware.
  • Shop & Warehouse Location Tracking: Know exact shelf locations for every item (`R1L1B1` for Shop counter shelves, `WR1L1B1` for Warehouse racks).
  • Customer Credit Ledger Management: Track credit sales to local mechanics and workshops, record balance payments, and view real-time customer balances.
  • Offline-First Reliability: Complete checkout and lookups continue without interruption even during internet outages.

Key Operational Features
