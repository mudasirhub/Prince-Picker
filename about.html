About Prince Picker

Prince Picker is a Point of Sale (POS) and inventory management platform designed specifically for two-wheeler and automotive spare-parts businesses, retailers, and warehouses.

What is Prince Picker?

Prince Picker is a specialized business application built to resolve the operational complexities of automotive parts distribution. It combines point-of-sale checkout, camera barcode scanning, multi-location inventory (Shop floor and Warehouse storage), vehicle model compatibility mapping, and customer credit ledger management into a single, offline-first system.

Who is it Designed For?

Prince Picker is built for:

  • Two-Wheeler Spare-Parts Retailers: Businesses selling parts for motorcycles and scooters (Honda, Hero, Bajaj, TVS, Yamaha, Suzuki, Royal Enfield).
  • Automotive Parts Distributors: High-SKU distributors managing thousands of part numbers, MRP/SP pricing, and manufacturer barcodes.
  • Warehouse Operators: Warehouses needing Rack/Level/Bin location tracking (`R1L1B1` / `WR1L1B1`) and fast order pick-and-drop workflows.

What Problems Does Prince Picker Solve?

  • High SKU & Vehicle Model Complexity: Automotive parts often fit multiple vehicle models. Prince Picker supports fitment groups to link compatible vehicle models to a single part record.
  • Network & Wi-Fi Dead Zones: Retail counters and physical warehouses often suffer from slow internet or connection dropouts. Prince Picker runs offline on local IndexedDB storage so billing and inventory checks never pause.
  • Shop Floor vs. Warehouse Discrepancies: Multi-location tracking keeps shop stock (`SHOP`) distinct from secondary warehouse stock (`WAREHOUSE`) with explicit location strings.

Core Platform Features

  • Offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) architecture with background data sync.
  • Native device camera barcode scanning for instant item lookup and billing.
  • Location-based inventory tracking (Shop `R1L1B1` and Warehouse `WR1L1B1`).
  • Vehicle compatibility mapping and fitment group management.
  • POS billing checkout, receipt generation, and customer credit ledger tracking.
  • Stock transfer management between shop floor and warehouse storage locations.

Where Can Users Access Prince Picker?

Prince Picker is accessible on web browsers, installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA), and optimized for Android mobile devices and tablets at https://princepicker.mudasirkhan.in/.
